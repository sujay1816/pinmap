WORTH A LOOK BEFORE YOU TRUST IT
--------------------------------
Adding the doctype moves the page from quirks mode into standards mode.
box-sizing was already border-box everywhere and nothing uses percentage
heights, so this should be invisible — but it is the one change here that
could shift the layout. Open the deployed page and glance at each of the
four screens.
